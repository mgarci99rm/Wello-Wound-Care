Frequently asked questions

Can’t find your answer? Call (833) 730-4498 or email hello@wellowoundcare.com.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Please contact us as soon as possible. We request 24-hour notice for routine appointments, so we can reallocate the visit; urgent changes will be managed case-by-case.

What should I prepare for my appointment?

Bring a list of current medications, recent wound notes or photos if available, and a means of contact for family or carers. For home visits, ensure a clean area for the clinician to work.

Are your clinicians credentialled?

Yes — our clinicians are board-certified or licenced in their disciplines, trained in modern wound therapies, and operate within established clinical governance frameworks.

What happens at the first visit?

We take a focused clinical history, map the wound, assess infection and risk factors, set measurable goals, and agree a practical plan. Expect clear explanations, modest pain control where needed, and a timeline for review.

How do I book an assessment?

Click the Book an appointment button, call us at (833) 730-4498, or fill out the contact form. We’ll ask a few quick questions and then arrange the best type of visit for you — either at home, in our clinic, or online.

Who can I contact for more information?

Use the site contact form, the dedicated booking button, or the phone number shown on our pages. For professional referrals, an email address for clinical liaison is available on request.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. We adhere to applicable data protection and health privacy standards. Notes are shared with the patient’s consent, and records are stored securely.
