Who we help

Wello cares for patients whose wounds:

  • Have less than 50% closure with traditional care
  • Haven’t healed in 30+ days
  • Are post-surgical, diabetic, or pressure-related
  • Require bedside treatment in ALF, SNF, hospice, or home

Our advanced graft therapy

Wello uses placental biologic grafts — ethically sourced from planned C-sections and rich in healing growth factors.

Regenerative materials that

  • Support collagen production
  • Speed tissue repair
  • Attract key healing cells
  • Reduce infection risk
  • Effective
  • Sterile
  • Less pain
  • Clinically approved
  • No clinic trip

What is graft therapy?

Graft therapy is the clinical placement of tissue or biologic material — autologous, donor-derived, or processed placental matrix — onto a damaged site to promote repair, reduce infection risk, and restore function.
