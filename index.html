Who does Wello assist?

We care for patients whose wounds:

  • Have less than 50% closure with traditional care
  • Haven’t healed in 30+ days
  • Are post-surgical, diabetic, or pressure-related
  • Require bedside treatment in ALF, SNF, hospice, or home

All FAQs and resources
What happens at the first visit?

We take a focused clinical history, map the wound, assess infection and risk factors, set measurable goals, and agree a practical plan. Expect clear explanations, modest pain control where needed, and a timeline for review.

How do I book an assessment?

Click the Book an appointment button, call us at (833) 730-4498, or fill out the contact form. We’ll ask a few quick questions and then arrange the best type of visit for you — either at home, in our clinic, or online.

What should I prepare for my appointment?

Bring a list of current medications, recent wound notes or photos if available, and a means of contact for family or carers. For home visits, ensure a clean area for the clinician to work.
