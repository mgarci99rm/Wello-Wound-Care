Privacy policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Wello Wound Care (“Wello,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses and protects information when you visit this website or contact us through it. It applies to this website only.

Your medical information

Health information we create or receive while providing care is protected by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and the California Confidentiality of Medical Information Act (CMIA, Cal. Civ. Code § 56 et seq.). How we use and share that information is described in our Notice of Privacy Practices, which is given to every patient and is available on request. Where that notice and this policy differ, the Notice of Privacy Practices controls for your medical information.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, phone number, email address, reason for contact, and anything you write in a message to us.
  • Communications: records of calls, emails and messages you exchange with us.
  • Technical information: like most websites, our hosting provider automatically receives basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, to deliver and secure the site. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels on this website.

Please don’t send detailed medical history, photos of wounds, insurance member numbers or other sensitive details through the website or by regular email. We’ll collect what we need securely when we speak with you.

2. How the contact form works

When you use “Send us a message,” the form prepares an email from your own email app, addressed to hello@wellowoundcare.com. Your details travel through your email provider and ours, and nothing is stored on this website. Standard email is not fully encrypted, which is why we ask you to keep medical details out of your message.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange an assessment or visit
  • To schedule, confirm, change or remind you about appointments
  • To verify insurance coverage and handle billing, with your permission
  • To coordinate with referring clinicians or care facilities, where you have asked us to
  • To operate, secure and improve this website
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations

4. How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We may share it only:

  • With service providers that help us run our practice (for example email, hosting, scheduling and billing services), who may use it only on our behalf
  • With your insurer or health plan, to confirm coverage and process claims
  • With other healthcare providers involved in your care, as permitted by HIPAA and the CMIA
  • When required by law, court order or a government authority, or to protect the rights and safety of patients, staff or others

5. How we protect your information

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal information. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention

We keep website enquiries only as long as needed to respond and to meet legal obligations. Medical records are kept for the periods required by California and federal law.

7. Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information online. A parent or legal guardian should contact us on behalf of a minor.

8. Do Not Track

As required by the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA), we note that this website does not track visitors across third-party websites over time, so it does not respond differently to “Do Not Track” browser signals.

9. Links to other websites

Our website may link to sites we don’t control. Their privacy practices are their own, so please review their policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we’ll change the effective date at the top of this page.

Your California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you may have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), to the extent they apply to Wello. Medical information governed by HIPAA or the CMIA is handled under those laws instead.

  • Right to know what personal information we have collected about you, where it came from, why we use it and who we share it with
  • Right to delete personal information we collected from you, subject to legal exceptions (for example, medical record retention requirements)
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information
  • Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information.
  • Right to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We use it only to provide the services you request.
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising any of these rights

Under California’s “Shine the Light” law (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.83), you may also ask whether we have shared personal information with third parties for their own direct marketing. We do not.

How to make a request: email hello@wellowoundcare.com or call (833) 730-4498. We will verify your identity before acting on a request, respond within 45 days as the law requires, and let you know if we need more time. You may use an authorized agent; we may ask for proof of their authorization.

For your medical records, you also have rights under HIPAA and California law to access, obtain copies of and request amendments to your records. Contact us and we will explain how.

Terms & conditions

1. Acceptance of terms

By using this website you agree to these terms. If you don’t agree, please don’t use the site.

2. Not medical advice — and not for emergencies

Content on this website is general information, not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Using the website or sending us a message does not create a patient–provider relationship; that begins only once we have assessed you and agreed to provide care. If you have a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. Do not use the contact form for urgent needs.

3. Services

Wello provides wound care at home, in clinic, in skilled nursing and long-term care settings, in hospice and palliative care, and by telehealth where appropriate. Availability depends on location, clinical suitability and scheduling. Treatments described on this website, including graft therapy, are offered only when clinically indicated, and results vary from patient to patient.

4. Appointments, cancellations and telehealth

We request 24-hour notice to cancel or reschedule routine appointments, so we can offer the visit to another patient. Urgent changes are handled case by case. Telehealth visits require your informed consent, which we will ask for before the first video visit, as California law requires.

5. Insurance and billing

We accept Medicare, Medi-Cal and many common plans. We will try to confirm coverage before care begins, but coverage decisions are made by your plan. You are responsible for any deductibles, co-payments or services your plan does not cover, and we will tell you about expected costs before non-covered services wherever possible.

6. Communications

If you give us your phone number or email, you agree that we may contact you about your enquiry, appointments and care. We will not send marketing messages without your consent, and you may ask us to stop at any time. Message and data rates may apply to text messages.

7. Intellectual property

The text, design, graphics and logos on this website belong to Wello Wound Care or its licensors and may not be copied or reused without permission.

8. Website accuracy and availability

We work to keep information on this site accurate and current, but we provide it “as is” and may change it at any time without notice. We don’t guarantee the site will always be available or error-free.

9. Limitation of liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, Wello Wound Care is not liable for any indirect, incidental or consequential damages arising from your use of this website. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited under California law, or our professional obligations to our patients.

10. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of California, without regard to its conflict-of-law rules.

Accessibility

If you have trouble using any part of the site, call (833) 730-4498 or email hello@wellowoundcare.com and we’ll help you get the information or service you need.
